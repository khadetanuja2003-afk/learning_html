This is about page

Nature is the physical world and its phenomena, including all living and non-living things that exist naturally and are not made by humans. It provides essential resources like air, water, and food, regulates the climate, and supports biodiversity through complex ecosystems. Nature is vital for human survival, health, and well-being, and is also a source of inspiration, though it faces threats from human activities like pollution and deforestation.
