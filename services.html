Mehndi Design Artist

Mehndi is the traditional art of applying body art using henna paste, which creates a temporary, stain-like design on the skin, typically on hands and feet. It is a significant ceremonial art form in many cultures, especially in South Asia and the Middle East, and is often associated with weddings, festivals, and other celebrations. Designs range widely, from intricate, full-hand patterns like Indian mehndi to more sparse, flowing styles like Arabic mehndi
